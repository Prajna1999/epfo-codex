### Simplified EPFO Claims Spec

Replace separate Form 31, 19, and 10C screens with one guided **Submit a claim** flow.

**Step 1 — Choose claim**

- PF advance
- Final PF settlement
- Pension benefit

**Step 2 — Confirm details**

Auto-fill and display:

- Name
- UAN
- Employer
- Joining/leaving dates
- Masked bank account

**Step 3 — Ask only relevant questions**

- PF advance: purpose, amount, purpose-specific details
- Final settlement: tax declaration, if applicable
- Pension benefit: withdrawal benefit or scheme certificate

**Step 4 — Review and submit**

- Show a concise summary
- Verify bank account
- Aadhaar OTP/e-sign
- Submit claim

Hide all office-use, duplicate, employer-certification, and legacy address fields. Generate the required Form 31/19/10C data automatically.

### Form 31 — PF advance / partial withdrawal

Used when you remain an EPFO member and want an advance for an eligible purpose. The official form includes:

- Mobile number
- Claim ID / office-use details
- Purpose of advance
- Amount requested
- Amount in words
- Member’s name
- Father’s name or husband’s name, where applicable
- Factory/establishment name and address
- PF account number
- Monthly basic wages
- Dearness allowance and total
- Full postal address
- Member’s signature/thumb impression

The purpose section changes depending on the reason—for example housing, illness, marriage, education, natural calamity, electricity cut, or equipment for a physically disabled member. Supporting declarations or certificates may be required for some purposes. [Official Form 31](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Downloads_PDFs/Form31.pdf), [EPFO Form 31 instructions](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Downloads_PDFs/TypesOfAdvances_Form31.pdf)

### Form 19 — final EPF settlement

Used for final settlement of the EPF balance. Its fields include:

- Mobile number
- Member’s name
- Father’s name or husband’s name
- Date of birth
- Last factory/establishment name and address
- PF account number
- UAN
- Date of leaving service
- Reason for leaving service
- PAN
- Whether Form 15G/15H is being submitted, where applicable
- Full postal address
- Payment method:
  - Postal money order, or
  - Account-payee cheque/electronic payment
- Savings bank account number
- Bank name
- Branch name and branch address
- IFSC code
- Date of joining the establishment
- Contribution for the current financial year
- Member’s declaration and signature
- Employer’s certification/signature, where applicable

The current official PDF shows the UAN, PAN, 15G/15H, payment and bank fields explicitly. [Official Form 19](https://www.epfindia.nic.in/site_docs/PDFs/Downloads_PDFs/Form19.pdf)

### Form 10C — EPS withdrawal benefit or scheme certificate

This concerns the Employees’ Pension Scheme, not the EPF balance itself. The main fields include:

- Mobile number
- Member’s name
- Claimant’s name(s), where applicable
- Date of birth
- Father’s name
- Husband’s name, where applicable
- Last establishment name and address
- Region/office code
- Establishment code
- Account number
- Date of joining the establishment
- Reason for leaving service
- Date of leaving service
- Full postal address
- Member’s signature/thumb impression
- Employer’s signature

The later sections of Form 10C can include:

- Whether the claimant is seeking withdrawal benefit or a scheme certificate
- Family/claimant particulars for applicable death-related claims
- Pension-service particulars
- Wages and non-contributory-service details
- Employer certification
- Payment/receipt details where applicable

The official form also states that withdrawal benefit is not admissible where eligible membership is under 180 days, excluding non-contributory service. [Official Form 10C](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Downloads_PDFs/Form10C.pdf)

In the current online flow, EPFO generally checks UAN status, date of joining/exiting, Aadhaar, bank account/IFSC and—where relevant—PAN before allowing Form 19, 10C or 31 submission.
